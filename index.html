Selected research

HomeRobot

How do you measure whether a robot can fetch an unfamiliar object? We built a benchmark that labs can run in simulated homes and on a real Stretch robot.

OK-Robot

We brought a mobile manipulator into ten real homes. Using pretrained models, it completed 58.5% of pick-and-drop tasks without training in those homes.

Stretch AI

An open-source toolkit for building on a Stretch. Give the robot a spoken instruction, let it explore a room, or teach it a new skill by demonstration.

Writing and conversations

On RoboPapers, we sit down with the authors of robotics papers to ask what worked, what broke, and how they know. I write about robot learning and the work of building robots at It Can Think.
